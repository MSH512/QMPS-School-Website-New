Principal's Message

Welcome to Quaid-e-Millat Public School Ameerabad

It is with great pride and joy that I welcome you to our school family. At Quaid-e-Millat Public School Ameerabad, we believe every child is a spark of potential waiting to be ignited. Our mission is simple yet powerful: to provide quality education that nurtures not just academic excellence, but also strong character, confidence, and a lifelong love for learning.

We are committed to shaping the young minds of today into the responsible, capable, and compassionate leaders of tomorrow. From modern classrooms to a wide range of co-curricular activities, we strive to offer holistic development opportunities for every child who walks through our doors.

I invite you to be a part of the QMPS Ameerabad family, where we don't just teach — we inspire, empower, and prepare young minds to shape a brighter future.
