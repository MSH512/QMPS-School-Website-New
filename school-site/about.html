The story of Quaid-e-Millat Public School Ameerabad — our founding, our mission, and the values that guide every classroom.

Our Journey

Quaid-e-Millat Public School Ameerabad was established in 1999 with a profound vision to provide quality education and foster a generation of leaders. The foundation of this esteemed institution was laid by one of the community's most honoured teachers, Malik M. Abdullah Hadi.

Driven by a passion for teaching and a commitment to societal upliftment, Mr. Hadi envisioned a school that would not only impart knowledge but also instill strong moral and ethical values. His dedication was to create an accessible, inclusive, and nurturing environment where students from all backgrounds could thrive.

Over the decades, the school has grown from a humble beginning into a beacon of learning in the region. We continue to uphold the principles set forth by our founder, adapting to modern educational practices while staying true to our core mission: to empower students to learn with purpose and leave ready to lead with integrity.

Who We Are

Since 1999, Quaid-e-Millat Public School Ameerabad has remained committed to providing high-quality, academically rigorous education that prepares students for lives of leadership, responsibility, and service to the community. Our goal is to nurture confident, knowledgeable, and morally strong individuals who are ready to meet the challenges of the future.

Each day at our school is filled with meaningful learning experiences that are both educational and enjoyable. From literacy and science to arts, co-curricular activities, and creative learning, we provide an environment where students can grow intellectually, socially, and personally.

Founded with the vision of making quality education accessible to the community of Ameerabad, the school began its journey as a small local institution and, over the years, has developed into a respected centre of learning known for excellence and dedication.

The name "Quaid-e-Millat" reflects the values of leadership, integrity, and service to the nation. We aim to inspire these same qualities in every student who becomes part of our institution.

We take pride in our dedicated teachers, hardworking staff, supportive parents, successful alumni, and well-wishers whose continued trust and cooperation help us maintain a positive and progressive learning environment. Together, we remain committed to shaping a brighter future for our students and our community.
